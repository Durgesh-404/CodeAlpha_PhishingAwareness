# Phishing Awareness Training & Defense Strategies

A comprehensive cybersecurity presentation deck on identifying social engineering tactics, email spoofing, and technical preventive controls. Developed for the CodeAlpha Cybersecurity Internship (Task 2).

## Content Overview
* **Introduction to Phishing**: Email phishing, Spear phishing, and Smishing/Vishing.
* **Technical Red Flags**: Typosquatting, suspicious URLs, and malicious attachments.
* **Social Engineering Tactics**: Authority, Urgency/Fear, Greed, and Pretexting.
* **Defensive Controls**: Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), out-of-band verification, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC protocols.
* **Interactive Knowledge Check**: Quiz questions evaluating phishing identification.
